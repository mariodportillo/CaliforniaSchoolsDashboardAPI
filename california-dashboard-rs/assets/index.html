Methodology and responsible interpretation

What the report calculates +

Reports use descriptive summaries of the API response: counts, observed values, changes supplied by the Dashboard, and explicit data-quality totals. They do not add causal claims, predictive scores, or an overall school ranking.

How missing and private data are handled +

Suppressed private values, unavailable measures, parse problems, and request failures are labeled separately. Missing values are excluded from numeric summaries and are never treated as zero or imputed.

How to make a sound comparison +

Compare the same indicator and student group across compatible years. Check denominators, coverage, suppression, and policy or measurement changes. Small observed differences can be practically unimportant and do not, on their own, imply a cause.

Data source and scope +

School identity comes from the local California public schools database. Indicator values come from the public California School Dashboard API. This independent tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by the State of California.
